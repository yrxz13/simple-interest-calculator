# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make participation welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our Standards

Treat others with respect, give constructive feedback, and listen to different perspectives. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and sharing another person’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers explain community standards and may remove harmful content or restrict participation when those standards are violated.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to a maintainer through the contact option on their GitHub profile. Include relevant context and avoid posting private details publicly. Maintainers will review reports and protect the reporter’s privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may request a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participation depending on the severity and history of the conduct.
